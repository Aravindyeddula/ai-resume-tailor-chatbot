AI Project: Customer Support Copilot

Overview:
This document describes a pilot AI assistant for customer support teams.

Main contribution:
The pilot introduced a retrieval-augmented assistant that reduced average first-response time from 14 minutes to 8 minutes during a 4-week trial.

Assumptions:
Support articles are updated weekly and written in English.
At least one human reviewer checks high-risk responses.

Limitations:
The assistant performs poorly on topics not covered in the knowledge base and may miss intent when users use slang.

Concrete numeric detail:
In week 3, the team processed 1,240 tickets and resolved 71% without escalation.
